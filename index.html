01 · THE IDEA

More than an AI assistant.

Athena is being developed as knowledge and intelligence infrastructure: a modular foundation where libraries, documents, retrieval, models, citations and workspace context can work together.

🧠

Knowledge Management

Organize information, libraries, metadata, collections and citations into a coherent local knowledge environment.

📄

Document Intelligence

Build a path from documents and source material toward structured, searchable and usable knowledge.

🔎

Retrieval & RAG

Retrieve relevant knowledge before generation, with measurable retrieval quality and application-level boundaries.

🤖

AI Provider Platform

Connect local and pluggable AI providers rather than locking the system to a single model or vendor.

🗂️

Workspace Intelligence

Bring context, projects, sessions and knowledge together into an environment prepared for intelligent workflows.

🔐

Local & Private

Designed around local-first operation and user-controlled infrastructure wherever practical.

03 · ENGINEERING JOURNEY

Built incrementally.

Athena's roadmap is organized as engineering stages, allowing the foundation to stabilize before increasingly intelligent capabilities are exposed.

A15StabilizationStrengthen the foundation and establish a dependable baseline.
A16AI Provider PlatformIntroduce a modular boundary for local and pluggable AI providers.
A17AI Control CenterExpose provider and model control through a coherent application layer.
A18Document IntelligenceMove from source documents toward structured knowledge workflows.
A19Retrieval IntelligenceDevelop measurable retrieval and application-level retrieval services.
A20Workspace IntelligenceExpose context and workspace intelligence through the user interface.
A21Plugin EcosystemExtend Athena through controlled integrations and capabilities.

05 · ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

Library First.
Code Second.

Local-first

Prefer user-controlled local infrastructure and offline-capable workflows.

Privacy-first

Keep knowledge and processing under the user's control wherever practical.

Integration-first

Connect capable components through clean boundaries instead of rebuilding everything.

Evidence-driven

Use benchmarks, tests and engineering audits to guide development.

Modular

Keep providers, retrieval, documents and workspace capabilities replaceable and extensible.
